Our story

Meet Miss Laura

Miss Laura's School of Dance opened its doors on August 1, 2012, with two dance rooms. Today the studio has grown to three rooms in Heritage Square, with 9 instructors plus seasonal instructors and choreographers.

The studio is run by Miss Laura and Mr Dan. Their promise is simple: quality dance education in an honest, respectful and welcoming environment, at affordable prices.

We offer a positive environment for children of any age, ability, gender, race, or socioeconomic status to learn the art of dance.
