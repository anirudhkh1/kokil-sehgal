Questions

How much does it cost?
Every project is different, so I don't use set packages. After our call, I send you a fixed quote, and that's the price you pay.
How long does it take?
A brand identity takes about 2 weeks, and a website is ready within 4 weeks. Social media setup takes about a week.
How do payments work?
For projects, you pay 40% to start, 30% at first draft and 30% at launch. Ongoing services are billed monthly. I invoice in USD, and you can pay by bank transfer or PayPal.
Will I own everything?
Yes. Once the final payment is made, you own all the files, your domain and your accounts.
What if I don't like the first draft?
That's what revisions are for. Three rounds are included, and I'll keep listening until it feels right.
Which platform do you build on?
I build on WordPress, Shopify or custom code. I'll recommend the right one for your business and how you want to update it.
Do you work with businesses outside the US and Canada?
Yes. Most of my clients are in the US, Canada, the UK and Australia.
What do you need from me to start?
Your logo and brand files if you have them, photos of your business, and your text or a rough outline. Examples of sites you like help too. If you're missing something, we'll figure it out together.
